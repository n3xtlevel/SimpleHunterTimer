# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Addon icon in the AddOns list.
- Fonts and bar texture: new Appearance page in the options. "Match my UI" (the default) uses ElvUI's or EllesmereUI's font and bar texture; you can also pick any font or texture from LibSharedMedia, plus font size and outline.
- Size settings moved to the Appearance page.
- Bars now sit flush against each other by default (they share a border line). A "Bar spacing" slider on the Appearance page adds a gap.

## 1.1.0

- Cast lane: a thin strip above the bar. Cyan while instant shots are safe, gold right after each shot (the moment to start Aimed Shot).
- Measuring: times how each ability affects your next Auto Shot. `/sht report` shows the results, `/sht resetstats` clears them. Abilities measured as restarting the timer get the gold window too, and if instants turn out harmless during the aim, the cyan extends through it.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Auto Shot bar with safe-to-move and aim zones, latency adjustment, a delay flash, an optional melee swing bar with Raptor Strike highlight, and a settings panel (/sht).
